# Harbor Wellness Design Brief

## Product Context
- **App type**: A single practice's responsive public information website with a minimal, no-account appointment-request flow. This is a brand-facing website, not a portal or real-time booking service.
- **Primary personas**: Prospective patients evaluating care and caregivers researching on someone else's behalf.
- **Primary jobs to be done**: Understand the practice and its care offerings, find practical visitor information, and request contact about an appointment without navigating clinical paperwork.

## Direction Summary
Propose **Grounded Welcome**: a typography-led, light website with warm paper surfaces, deep plum emphasis, and clear humanist lettering. A prospective patient or caregiver may be reading on a phone in daylight between other tasks, so legibility and a direct path to contact matter more than immersive decoration. Broad left-aligned headings, open service rows, and quiet horizontal rules create a recognizable identity without a stock-photo hero or elaborate assets. This is a designer-selected proposal following permission to choose, not a previously selected brand or reference style.

## Brand and Mood
- **Brand personality**: Plainspoken, considerate, grounded, quietly confident.
- **Mood**: An approachable public front door that gives visitors room to decide, without luxury-spa positioning or clinical-system density.
- **Keywords**: Welcoming, legible, composed, honest, uncomplicated.

## Color System
- **Backgrounds / neutrals**: Warm paper `oklch(97% 0.008 75)` for the page; porcelain `oklch(99% 0.004 75)` for inputs and necessary panels; pale stone `oklch(94% 0.009 75)` for occasional section differentiation. Ink `oklch(25% 0.015 320)` and muted ink `oklch(44% 0.015 320)` provide text hierarchy. Use `oklch(83% 0.010 75)` for decorative rules and `oklch(57% 0.018 320)` for control boundaries.
- **Primary accent**: Deep plum `oklch(38% 0.085 325)`, reserved principally for request actions, links, and a small number of brand details. This is a restrained palette, not a full-page purple drench. Hover/pressed tones are `oklch(33% 0.080 325)` and `oklch(29% 0.070 325)`.
- **Secondary accents**: Pale plum `oklch(94% 0.018 325)` may support an informational band or active state; no competing decorative accent color.
- **Semantic colors**: Success `oklch(37% 0.075 155)`, warning `oklch(42% 0.085 75)`, error `oklch(40% 0.140 25)`, and information via primary plum. Use words and optional small symbols as well as color. Verify rendered contrast before launch: 4.5:1 for normal text, 3:1 for large text and essential control/focus boundaries. No pure black or white.

## Typography Direction
- **Display / emphasis**: Source Sans 3, weight 600, upright and left-aligned. Its humanist shapes suit clear visitor signage rather than editorial luxury. Use the same family for the simple Harbor Wellness text wordmark; do not invent a practice emblem.
- **Body / UI text**: Source Sans 3, weight 400 for prose and 600 for labels/actions, with `system-ui, sans-serif` fallback. Prefer locally hosted font files and `font-display: swap`; no required remote font service.
- **Hierarchy notes**: Body 18px with 1.55 line height; form text at least 16px; supporting text 16px. Heading steps approximately 24, 32, 40, and a fluid hero 40–64px. Hero line height 1.08, other headings 1.2; headline width around 18–22 characters and prose around 60–68 characters. Use sentence case, no all-caps paragraphs, decorative italics, or very light weights.

## Layout and Navigation
- **Layout direction**: Spacious but compact enough to understand the offering quickly. Mobile-first container up to 1120px, gutters 20px on phones, 32px on tablets, and 48px on wide screens. Spacing scale: 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 88px. Section spacing ranges from 48px on narrow screens to 88px on wide screens; related text and controls stay tightly grouped.
- **Navigation pattern**: Non-sticky public top navigation with the practice name linked home, a Services link, and a clear Request an appointment action. Keep practical visitor details and FAQs within the approved public content, using section links rather than adding new destinations. At approximately 720px, or when labels no longer fit, use a labeled Menu button opening an inline navigation region below the header. Expose expanded state, support Escape, and return focus to the trigger when closed from inside; no full-screen drawer or overlay. Never add sign-in links, sidebar chrome, or a separate caregiver interface.
- **Screen rhythm**: Home opens with a generous left-aligned headline and concise explanation, one primary request action, and a quiet services link. On wide screens, pair this with a narrower text-only explanation of the request process, not a pretend photo panel; stack in reading order on phones. Follow with open service summaries separated by rules, practice-approved visitor guidance, and a clear contact prompt. Services content uses readable sections or rows rather than identical icon cards. Request content uses a calm single column, maximum 600px, with its explanation and outcome in the same site shell. Footer carries only approved practical details and available navigation. At 320px width and 200% zoom, no horizontal page scrolling or clipped controls.

## Component Style
- **Surfaces**: Mostly unboxed content and 1px horizontal separators. Use pale surface panels only when grouping a form or feedback genuinely helps. Controls have 8px corner radii; necessary panels up to 12px. No nested cards, shadows as decoration, gradients, or colored side stripes.
- **Inputs and forms**: Persistent labels above native inputs, at least 48px control height, and 20–24px between fields. Collect the requestor's name, preferred contact method, the corresponding email or phone contact detail, and optional broad service interest with an undecided option. A caregiver supplies their own contact details; do not add patient identity or relationship questions. Use channel selection to avoid requiring both email and phone. Broad service choices must come from approved content or be clearly labeled preview placeholders. No notes box, symptoms, medical history, insurance, dates, availability selection, attachments, or clinical intake. Brief helper copy states that this is a request for contact, not a confirmed appointment, and asks visitors not to submit medical information.
- **Buttons and actions**: Plum filled primary button with porcelain text, minimum 48px height. Use Request an appointment on information pages and Send request on the form. Secondary navigation is a visible text link; links in prose are underlined. Full-width submit on small screens; natural width elsewhere. Keep disabled states readable and explain why an action cannot run; do not disable submit merely because initial fields are empty.
- **Data display**: Plain-language service rows, compact lists, and practical information blocks. No dashboards, tables, metrics, invented reviews, outcome claims, badges, or provider directory. FAQs, if used in existing public sections, can use native disclosure controls with visible focus.

## Motion and Feedback
- **Motion tone**: Minimal, functional, and calm. Color/opacity transitions around 140–180ms with ease-out; no scroll choreography, parallax, autoplay, bounce, or animated layout. Respect reduced-motion preferences.
- **Interaction notes**: Every control has distinct default, hover, focus, pressed, and disabled treatment. Focus is a 3px plum outline with 3px offset; on a plum button use a porcelain inner separation so the ring remains visible. Touch targets are at least 44px, preferably 48px; hover never reveals essential content. Provide a skip link and semantic landmarks/headings.
- **Form feedback**: Validate touched fields on blur and the whole form on submit, not prematurely on every keystroke. Name is required without restrictive name-pattern assumptions. The selected contact channel is required and checked for plausible formatting; allow international phone input. Display specific errors beneath fields, associate helper/error text programmatically, and identify the first invalid field with focus after a failed submit. Preserve entered values on validation or transport failure in page memory, not persistent browser storage.
- **Submission lifecycle**: Sending request… replaces the submit label during an actual request and blocks duplicate presses; announce pending/error/success accessibly. Only a positive server acknowledgment of a durably accepted request may show Request received. Receipt copy says the practice will contact the visitor and explicitly says no appointment has been booked; do not promise response timing unless supplied. Do not show receipt based on a timer, optimistic UI, or simply opening a confirmation URL. If submission is unavailable or fails, retain the form and show an honest retryable error, never a fake success. Avoid echoing contact information into the receipt URL or public display. Offer Back to home without creating an account.

## Anti-Patterns / Constraints
- No stock stethoscope photography, anonymous clinician/spa imagery, generic teal wellness templates, gradient heroes, glowing CTAs, testimonial carousels, or identical icon-card grids. No newly required imagery; typography, spacing, and color carry this simple site. Future supplied photography must be authentic, approved, and non-identifying unless authorized.
- Harbor Wellness is the roadmap's working practice identity. Use clearly marked neutral preview content until practice-approved descriptions, service names, contact details, location, hours, and follow-up expectations exist. Do not invent actual clinicians, credentials, specialties, addresses, testimonials, service availability, health outcomes, or compliance claims. Do not turn placeholder contact details into actionable links; verified content is required before public launch.
- Remain within the approved public information and general-request surfaces. Do not design accounts, admin tools, a booking calendar, payments, integrations, clinical intake, messaging, or MFA.
- Preserve the existing user-selected Specode PNG favicon and the Secure HttpOnly authentication scaffold without authentication changes. Public navigation must not expose or depend on that scaffold.
- Accessibility target: WCAG 2.2 AA, including contrast, keyboard operation, clear error identification, mobile reflow, visible focus, reduced motion, and screen-reader announcements. These are design requirements, not a compliance certification.
